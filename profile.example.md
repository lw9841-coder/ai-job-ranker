# Scoring rubric (score every job, using integers 1-5)

**they_pick_him: would a hiring manager move the candidate to an interview?**
- **5:** Meets every must-have, including years.
- **4:** Meets most must-haves; the gap can be bridged with related experience.
- **3:** One notable gap, such as years in a specific title or one named tool.
- **2:** Several gaps, or 5+ years in a specific function.
- **1:** A hard blocker: a strictly required degree, clearance, license, or language.

**he_wants_it: does the job match what the candidate wants?**
- **5:** Right kind of work, right location, pay in range.
- **3:** Mixed: only part of it fits.
- **1:** Wrong location, pay under range, or pure cold sales.

**job_quality: is it a good job overall?**
- Pay against the target range, company health (ratings, recent layoffs), and room to grow.
- 5 = strong pay and a healthy company. 3 = average or unknown. 1 = low pay or a troubled company.

**Rules**
- Quote the posting to back each requirement, 15 words or fewer per quote.
- No estimates. A number comes from the posting, or it is written "not listed".
